Title: Bramert Medical opens Tübingen production facility in partnership with Helvantis hospital group
URL: https://www.bramert-medical.example/press/tuebingen-facility-2026
Date: 2026-07-14

Tübingen, Germany. Bramert Medical, a European medical device manufacturer of
infusion systems and fluid management equipment, today opened a new 12,000
square metre production facility in Tübingen. The site was developed in
partnership with the Helvantis hospital group, which has signed a long-term
supply agreement for Bramert's volumetric infusion pumps and disposable sets.

The expansion adds three automated assembly lines and a cleanroom for disposable
manufacture, and is expected to create around 140 jobs in the region over the
next three years. Bramert says the investment strengthens its supply position in
the German, Austrian and Swiss hospital markets, where demand for infusion
equipment has grown steadily since 2024.

"Local manufacture close to our largest customers shortens lead times and gives
hospitals certainty of supply," said Chief Operations Officer Markus Bramert.
"The Helvantis partnership anchors the site from day one."

Bramert Medical was founded in 1987 and employs around 900 people across four
European sites. Its quality organisation holds long-standing certifications for
its full device portfolio, and the company runs established engineering and
regulatory teams at its Stuttgart headquarters.
