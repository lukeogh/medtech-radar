Title: Quellin Diagnostics raises €8.5 million seed round to industrialise its Raman-on-chip test platform
URL: https://www.quellindx.example/news/seed-round-2026
Date: 2026-07-16

Leuven, Belgium. Quellin Diagnostics, a spin-off from imec and Ghent University,
today announced an €8.5 million seed round led by Meridian DeepTech Capital with
participation from Fenlo Ventures and Arivo Health Capital. The company is
developing a photonics-based in vitro diagnostic (IVD) platform that uses
integrated Raman spectroscopy on a silicon chip to quantify small-molecule
biomarkers in whole blood within minutes, at the point of care.

The round will fund the move from laboratory prototype to a manufacturable
cartridge and reader, the expansion of the microfluidics team, and the company's
first software, quality and regulatory hires. Quellin currently employs eleven
people, all photonics, chemistry and microfluidics specialists. The company has
no software team yet and plans to build one over the coming year as it prepares
its development processes for the regulated pathway ahead.

"Our chip does in one measurement what a central lab does in hours," said
Dr Annelies Verhoest, co-founder and CEO. "This round lets us turn a working
sensor into a clinical product. The next phase is as much about disciplined
engineering and quality systems as it is about photonics."

Quellin was founded in 2025 based on a decade of integrated photonics research.
The platform targets therapeutic drug monitoring and critical care panels, with
a first application in antibiotic dosing for intensive care patients across
European hospitals.
