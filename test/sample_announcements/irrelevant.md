Title: Voltaneo Electronics unveils Aurel X wireless earbuds with adaptive spatial audio
URL: https://www.voltaneo.example/newsroom/aurel-x-launch
Date: 2026-07-15

San Jose, California. Consumer electronics brand Voltaneo Electronics today
unveiled the Aurel X, its flagship wireless earbuds featuring adaptive spatial
audio, on-device noise cancellation and a claimed 40 hours of battery life with
the charging case.

The Aurel X uses Voltaneo's third-generation audio processor and a redesigned
driver that the company says delivers deeper bass at lower power. A companion
app offers personalised sound profiles, gaming mode with reduced latency, and
integration with major voice assistants. The earbuds ship in four colours from
September at a recommended retail price of $249.

"Aurel X is the best-sounding product we have ever made," said Dana Ferro,
Voltaneo's head of consumer products. "We tuned every part of the experience,
from the first pairing to the last percent of battery."

Voltaneo Electronics designs audio and smart home entertainment products sold
in over forty countries. The Aurel X range will be showcased at the company's
autumn hardware event alongside updates to its soundbar line.
