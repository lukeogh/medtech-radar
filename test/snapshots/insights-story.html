A university in Enschede spins out Selwick Photonics with a €5m seed round to build a handheld Raman reader for wound infection

Relevance 93 · Spin-off launch · Found 12 Nov · Reported by 4 sources

On your Watchlist: Selwick Photonics, open it on the Watchlist

Summary

Selwick Photonics, spun out of the university's optics group this month, has closed a seed round of €5 million led by Delta Seed Fund. The company is building a handheld Raman reader that tells a district nurse within minutes whether a wound is infected, without sending a swab to a laboratory. Its first clinical study starts in spring.

In the words of Lowlands photonics cluster, the first report the watcher found.

Why it matters

New spin-off at seed stage with a software-driven diagnostic reader and no software or quality hires announced.
