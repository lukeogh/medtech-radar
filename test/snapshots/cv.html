Scoring preferences

What a role is judged against, beside the CV. They live in test/snapshot_profile/preferences.md, which stays out of git.

Changing these changes how roles are scored. The scorer reads them for every role it scores from the next one on. A change to a floor, the pass mark or the Filtered floor also re-sorts the roles already scored, in code, at no cost, and says how many moved. A change to location or sector cannot be worked out again without the model, so saving one offers a paid rescore, with the count and the cost first.

£ a day

A day rate under this fails the Rate gate. A range is judged on its top figure; an hourly rate counts times eight.

Rate gate · re-sorted in code

£ a year

A salary under this fails the Rate gate. A salary is judged as a salary and never turned into a day rate.

Rate gate · re-sorted in code

CV match

A CV match at the pass mark or over passes the CV gate. Under the Filtered floor a role is Filtered: counted on Jobs, never listed.
