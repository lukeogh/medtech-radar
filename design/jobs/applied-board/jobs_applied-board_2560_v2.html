Annotations

Jobs, the Applied view laid out as a Board, at #jobs?view=applied&layout=board. Friday 13 Nov 2026 09:14; times are UK time. Every company, person, role and file name is invented. Stages, the 21-day rule, next steps and Waiting on you are proposals: today Radar stores only the date applied.

  1. 1

    Show: Applied, then List or Board

    The Applied view keeps round-2 Jobs' header and Show facet; a List | Board switch is added, Board current. The facet counts what is still open, Preparing to Offer: 10. Closed items stay on the board, 6 of them, and are not counted. The header's 6 new counts unread roles in every tier, Reading included; Home's figures count only new Top prospects and One question away.

  2. 2

    Waiting on you

    The items with a next step due, soonest first, each a link to its item: Follow up with Wrenfield Digital Health, due today; Reply to Calder Neurotech, due tomorrow; Prepare for the Severn Renal Systems interview, by Mon 16 Nov, interview Tue 17 Nov 10:00. Home's applications tile lists the same three.

  3. 3

    Filled by Radar

    Radar fills Preparing (a tailored CV is saved, the role not applied) and Applied (marked applied on Jobs or Prepare application) by itself, and both heads say so. Every later column is the owner's.

  4. 4

    An item

    The title is the link that opens the role; the button beside it is separate. Then the date applied and the company, the CV file that was sent, the days in this column and the next step, if any. Divided rows, not boxed cards, as design-system §7 asks.

  5. 5

    Move to, one click

    Each item's 32px Move to button (key M), named by its role, lists the later columns, each one click and keyed by its number (2 to 5): here on “Software Quality Lead, respiratory devices”. Closed comes once for each reason, keyed N, T or W, so a close always says why. There is no dragging: every move is this menu or a key.

  6. 6

    The 21-day rule, seen ahead

    An Applied item with no movement for 14 days or more says when Radar will close it as no reply, so the move never comes as a surprise. Radar closes it without asking; Undo and Move back to Applied put it back.

  7. 7

    Closed by Radar

    After 21 days with no movement an Applied item closes itself as no reply and says so: “Moved here by Radar on 12 Nov after 21 days with no movement”. One click on Move back to Applied puts it back.

  8. 8

    Why it closed

    Every Closed item carries its reason as a word and a shape (a clock, an x, a turn-back arrow), never colour alone, and who closed it and when. The head counts each reason.

  9. 9

    An empty column says what lands there

    Offer is empty: it says an item lands there when the owner moves it after an offer, and that Radar never moves one there.

  10. 10

    Keys

    Arrow keys move between items, Enter opens the role, M opens Move to; in it 2 to 5 picks a column and N, T or W closes with that reason, the same keys the menu shows. Z undoes the last move. Hidden under 1024px with every key hint.

  11. 11

    Phone: one column at a time

    Six links with their counts, three to a row, pick the column shown; items stack under it at full width, so nothing scrolls sideways. Moves use the same Move to button, 44px tall here.

  12. 12

    Undo

    Every move gives an Undo notice: “Moved “IEC 62304 Consultant” to In conversation.” Z does the same, and Undo last in the header reaches further back.
