Direction notes

Rationale

A standing view of the market; scanning and comparing gates across many roles at once.

Borrowed from

IBM Carbon data tables, NN/g table guidance, Sales Navigator Account Hub.

Trade-offs

Best for scanning, comparing and auditing; slower per-role decisions; risk of spreadsheet fatigue; Prepare application opens a full page that fetches and scores the full description once.

Pages this changes most

Jobs, Home (a one-screen summary of counts and short lists, as text), Watchlist (a company table by last signal).

Primary action and why

Prepare application in the expanded row, because expanding a role is the moment of deciding to pursue it.

Proposals this screen shows

Saved filters, the expiring count and label, bulk actions, Park until…, the Provisional label, reasons on a card score, tier group rows with Show more, the renamed buttons, the Watchlist page in the nav. With nothing checked, the toolbar holds "Search roles" (focused by /), facet chips Tier, Region, Type and State, and Columns and Density.
