Watchlist: notes

The final Watchlist, from variation A, Table and record. Drawn for Friday 13 Nov 2026, 09:14. Frames: 2560, 1280, phone, phone-record, states.

Rationale

Alex chooses the companies; Radar only suggests. So the page has two lists that never mix: the companies Alex watches, in a table, and Radar's suggestions, folded to one dashed line above the table ("3 suggestions waiting, Show") below 2560 and open in dashed cards under it at 2560, each with Add to Watchlist and Dismiss. A suggestion becomes a row only when it is added. A company can also arrive by name, from the field in the header, or straight from Insights, where Add to Watchlist saves at once; the next visit says so and labels the row.

The table answers "who needs me now": threads due sort first, and the thread column leads with when it is due. The record opens beside the table so the reason and the next step sit next to the row, and it stays in view as the table scrolls (sticky, 16 pixels from the top, scrolling inside itself; the 1280 frame is drawn as an 800-pixel-high window, so its lower parts are in the HTML, the 2560 frame and the phone record). Inside the record the work comes first (the thread, the drafts, Log a contact, the contacts) and the reference after (signals and stories, roles on Jobs, the facts as published). At 2560 the two halves stand side by side.

From variation A, and what changed

  • Kept: the table with a written order line, the record beside it with Previous and Next on K and J, counts in words, the contacts newest first with their outcome, signals and roles lists, Not known for a missing fact, and Log a contact writing the next step and due date.
  • Changed: suggestions are kept apart, folded to a strip or in cards of their own, with Dismiss (A named one company in a note); the state labels are plain words (Not contacted, Contacted, Working with them, Not pursuing); adding by name is new; the comment and connection-note drafts with Copy and Log a contact move here from the Insights story page (A said no drafts); each fact carries its source and date; People holds only a published chief executive and chief technology officer, as enrich_company keeps (A showed a VP Quality); the record sits beside the table from 1024 (A: from 1800).
  • Dropped: A's Mark the signal done box in the contact form. The signal's own Done already logs a touch and books week three (set_signal_state), so ticking both in one save would log two contacts.
  • Hiring stays off Insights (since 15 Nov). The buying window is listed in the record as a signal that opens on Jobs, and the company's roles are listed as Jobs draws them.

Keyboard

  • J and K move through the table and the record follows; Enter opens the record and moves focus to its heading; Esc closes it and returns focus to the row; L moves focus to Log a contact's first field; / moves focus to Add a company by name. Every one is also a button or link.
  • Single-key shortcuts can be turned off: the "Single-key shortcuts: On" switch beside the keys list (a switch, remembered on this device). They are suspended while focus is in a field, so typing a J or a / in a note never moves anything.
  • Add by name is a combobox: typing filters, Up and Down choose, Enter adds, Esc closes the list and then clears the field. The input carries aria-activedescendant naming the chosen option, so focus stays in the field. Set state is a menu button (In conversation, Working with them, Not pursuing): arrows move, Enter sets, Esc closes and returns focus to the button.
  • Undo after Accept, Dismiss and Remove takes focus, and stays until the page is left. Copy, logging and every result are said in a polite live region. The phone has no keys. Buttons are 32 pixels high everywhere, Change and Edit included, so every target clears the 24-pixel minimum of WCAG 2.2 (target size, minimum); the phone's Back link is 44.

Accessibility

Landmarks: the bar's navigation, main, and beside the table the record as a complementary region named by its heading, which takes tabindex -1 so Enter and J and K can move focus to it; the record scrolls inside itself, and everything in it is reachable by Tab. Headings run h1 Watchlist, h2 for Your companies, Suggested by Radar and the company's name, h3 for each part of the record. On the phone record the company's name is the h1 and the record is the page's article, not a complementary region. The table has a caption with the order, column headers and the company as row header; the selected row has aria-current. Due today, overdue, no date and new are each a shape and words (a clock, a filled triangle, a dash, a star), never colour alone. Fields are labelled, the due date's error is tied to its field with aria-describedby and aria-invalid.

Build needs

  • /touch/log takes the whole contact. The touches table already has next_action, next_action_date and outcome (db/schema.sql; touch.py and the signal Done booking write them), but the route writes only channel and note, stamped now. It needs the date, the outcome, the next step and its due date, and must refuse a next step without a date.
  • How a company joined. companies.watched_at says when, not how. A watched_via column (insights, suggestion, by hand) gives the arrival label and Added from Insights.
  • Suggestions. A watch_suggestions table (company, the signal behind it, suggested, accepted and dismissed times) and routes to accept, dismiss and undo, filled after each watcher run.
  • Add by name. A route that looks the name up by normalise_company among companies, offers matches, and for an unknown name calls resolve_company and sets watched_at.
  • A source for each fact. enrich_company stores one date (enriched_at) and one status for the whole company (ok when the site was read, text-only otherwise). The record's per-fact source needs the enricher to return a source with each field.
  • Threads due for the Watchlist only. build_dashboard counts every company's newest contact; the figure on Home and the table's tags should share one query limited to watched companies.
  • Edit and delete a contact, and Move the date and Clear the next step on a thread. Only the outcome can be changed today (/touch/outcome).
  • A connection note after a logged contact. Drafts are written today only when a story arrives (signals.draft_note). The 30 Oct note, written after Alex's comment on Rosa Okoye's post, needs a draft written when a comment is logged (one model call), stored against the company.
  • The page and its routes. GET /watchlist and /watchlist?company=<id> (record beside the table), /watchlist/<id> on a phone, and On your Watchlist on Insights pointing here instead of /company/<id>.

Where the brief and the code differ

  • The brief says the touches table has no next step or due date. It has both (next_action, next_action_date); only the /touch/log route ignores them. Drawn as the brief asks, listed above as a route change rather than a new column.
  • The brief names the colour tokens --ok, --warn and --bad; FRAME_CSS calls them --positive, --caution and --negative. These frames use FRAME_CSS's names.
  • Threads due match Home final: Qurio overdue and Lumenhal Photonics due today (last contact 23 Oct), with Hartwell's next step due Sun 15 Nov. Home final names the VP Quality as Hartwell's contact; this page has Rosa Okoye, the CTO, since the enricher keeps only a published chief executive and chief technology officer.
  • PDX Diagnostics is based in Leiden (the brief), and its suggestion here says so; the role Home final shows is at its Cambridge site, which this page does not show.
  • Home final's buying window comes from the Head of Software Quality advert (11 Nov); the brief's role for Prepare application is the Software Quality Lead contract, first seen 21 Oct, before the first contact on 30 Oct. Both roles are drawn on Jobs, and the buying window is the later one, which is the only one that can be a buying window.

Open questions

  1. 1. What may Radar suggest?

    Drawn: Companies you do not watch with a buying window (a first quality, regulatory or software hire at a company you have contacted), a funding round, or any Insights story at 70 or more. Each card says which and links to it.

    Alternatives: Buying windows only (fewest, strongest); any story at 40 or more (the digest's bar, many more cards); funding rounds and buying windows but not other stories.

  2. 2. How often, and how many at once?

    Drawn: Checked after each watcher run; at most five waiting, the strongest first; a suggestion not answered in 14 days goes quietly into the dismissed fold.

    Alternatives: Once a week with the Monday digest; no cap; suggestions wait until answered.

  3. 3. Do dismissed suggestions come back?

    Drawn: Not for the same signal. A later signal of a stronger kind (a buying window or a funding round) can bring it back once, marked dismissed before. The fold keeps 90 days, each with Suggest again.

    Alternatives: Never again; back after 90 days whatever happens; back on any new signal.

  4. 4. Is a next step required when logging a contact?

    Drawn: No. Leave it empty and the thread closes. A next step does need a due date, so every open thread can fall due.

    Alternatives: Required on every contact; a next step without a date allowed, as touch.py allows today (it shows No date set and never counts on Home); a default date a week out filled in.

  5. 5. Which threads count on Home?

    Drawn: Only Watchlist companies'. A thread for a company you removed or never added stays on file but is not counted.

    Alternatives: Every company's newest contact with a next step, as build_dashboard counts today (Okara would make it 3).

  6. 6. Does removing a company close its thread?

    Drawn: No. The thread stays on file and stops counting; adding the company again brings it back as it was.

    Alternatives: Removing clears the next step; removing asks first when a thread is open.

  7. 7. What does adding an unknown name do?

    Drawn: Adds a company row with no facts. They stay Not known until a story or advert names it and the enricher reads it then (one model call, as today, never from the name alone).

    Alternatives: Refuse names Radar has not seen; run the enricher on the name at once (a paid call, and it would have to guess the site, which enrich_company refuses to do).

  8. 8. Which drafts does the record show?

    Drawn: The comment and connection note from the newest story that has drafts, labelled with that story and its date.

    Alternatives: Every story's drafts in a list; a fresh connection note written for the current next step (a paid call per click).

  9. 9. Is the record beside the table at 1280?

    Drawn: Yes, from 1024 wide: the table drops to three columns and the record is 560 wide, and it stays in view as the table scrolls (it scrolls inside itself). On a phone it is its own view with Back to Watchlist.

    Alternatives: Variation A's rule: beside the table only from 1800, its own page below that.

  10. 10. Do facts ever refresh?

    Drawn: No: read once, as enrich_company promises, each with its source and date, so a stale fact shows its age.

    Alternatives: A Read again button (one model call and one polite fetch); refresh when a new funding round arrives.

  11. 11. What do the state labels say?

    Drawn: Plain words for the states Radar works out or Alex sets: Not contacted (Radar has seen stories or adverts, no contact yet), Contacted, Buying window open, In conversation, Working with them and Not pursuing.

    Alternatives: The code's own words (seen, touched, window open, in conversation, client, dead); no state column at all, since the thread already says what matters.

  12. 12. Where do the suggestions sit?

    Drawn: Below 1920 wide they fold to one line above the table (3 suggestions waiting, Show), so a company row is on the first screen; at 2560 they sit open under the table.

    Alternatives: Always open above the table (round one's choice); a separate Suggestions tab.
