# Design hand-off, round 1

Every frame on the Superdesign canvas [MedTech Radar redesign, round 1](https://superdesign.dev/teams/2140dc78-b239-499a-ac98-8022c63d00c3/projects/0db15a19-3516-4453-a7e9-67ecc19e8823), exported on 14 Nov 2026,
grouped by page and then by variation. A variation that covers both Prepare application and CV has its screens
split between those two folders under the same name.

**Files.** Each frame has three files side by side:

- a PNG of the whole page at the frame's width, full height (frames taller than Superdesign's 10,000px limit
  were taken whole from their preview links). App screens are drawn at a fixed viewport, as on the canvas, so
  content further down a scrolling pane is in the HTML, not the PNG;
- the draft's HTML, as Superdesign stores it (older versions: the exact file that was imported as that version);
- the CSS the page renders with: its own `<style>` blocks, then the rules the Tailwind CDN generated (the two
  blocks Superdesign's preview host injects are left out).

**Names.** `<page>_<variation>_<view>_v<version>`. The view is the width (`2560`, `1280`, `phone`, and `1440` for
round one) or `states` for a board of states, with a qualifier where a page has more than one frame at a width
(`2560-advert-read`, `1280-applied`, `phone-list`, `1280-company`). Shared pages are `shared_<what>_<width>_v<version>`.

**Status.** final: the agreed design. reviewed: drawn, checked by reviewers and fixed (or, for round one's
before frame, today's page used as the reviewed baseline), waiting for a pick. unreviewed: drawn and
self-checked by its author only (Home, Insights and the watchlist skipped their review round; round one's first
drafts came before its audit). **Current** marks the latest version of a frame, which is what the canvas shows;
older versions sit beside it. Superdesign keeps a draft's height, so a frame that grew taller got a separate
draft on the canvas for its new version: the Jobs phone list (v2), the Jobs notes (v2 and v3) and the 2560 and
1280 annotated Jobs frames (v2).

**Update, 14 Nov.** Every round-2 Jobs frame has a new current version with three changes. The four gate marks
on each queue row are labelled: an S C L R column header (Sector, CV match, Location, Rate) sits above them, and
each mark has a tooltip and an accessible name, such as "Rate fails, £650/day is under the £700 floor", with
the tick and cross kept. Acknowledge (key A) replaces Archive, and Mark applied (key M) is used everywhere. Each
row's date is labelled as the first-seen date ("First seen 21 Oct"); the role pane still shows when the role
was last seen. The Applied view looks the same, as its rows have no gate marks and already say when the role
was applied; only its stylesheet changed. The versions before the change stay beside them, marked older.

**Update, 14 Nov evening: Home round 2 and the applications board.** Round one's Home options were text lists;
round 2 makes Home a dashboard in the round-2 Jobs frame, dark. Every chart answers one question written as its
subtitle, shows its numbers beside the marks, labels everything in words, never relies on colour alone, and is
plain SVG and CSS with nothing loaded from the internet. Three variations, no winner picked:

- **A, Stacked band** (`home/round-2/home_round-2_a-*`): a welcome strip, four tiles across, then "Needs you
  today" with its detail pane. One glance at the shape of the day before any work; the band takes height from
  the list on every visit, most at 1280.
- **B, Work and rail** (`home_round-2_b-*`): the welcome and the list in the wide column, five tiles in a rail
  (two columns at 2560), and the only map. The work keeps a steady height; the tiles are read as a column, and
  at 1280 the rail scrolls to reach health and the map.
- **C, Welcome hero and bento** (`home_round-2_c-*`): a warmer, taller welcome with four large count links and
  the health lights folded in, a bento row sized by importance, then the list. A calm first screen; the work
  comes third, and at 1280 the page scrolls to the list.

The full applications board is in `jobs/applied-board/` (2560 and phone): six columns, Radar fills Preparing
and Applied, every move one click by button or key, and the 21-day "no reply" rule says so and can be
undone. `home_round-2_states_v1` holds the quiet day, the first week, no applications yet and a Radar fault;
`home_round-2_notes_v1` holds each variation's rationale and trade-offs, the board's, and the open questions.
The welcome reads "Good morning, Alex", the mockups' fictional identity; the live page would use the owner's
first name. These frames had one review pass (two reviewers) and its findings were fixed.

**Update, 15 Nov.** A second study for Insights, as a news front page for a weekly read: three variations (A Front page, B Broadsheet, C Edition), each drawn at 2560, 1280 and phone, with a story page and a states board (a quiet week, a busy week, the watcher failing), plus a shared notes frame with rationale, trade-offs and open questions. They are hand-drawn HTML in the dark frame built on `claude/jobs-redesign`, not Superdesign drafts, and sit under [insights/news](#insightsnews). **B, Broadsheet, is the pick** (15 Nov); A and C stay beside it as the options not taken.

**Counts.** 148 frames exported (444 files), the 25 in insights/news among them. 4 older versions could
not be exported; they are listed under jobs with the reason. All sample data is fictional. Round one's
direction notes address the owner by first name.

### insights/news

Round 1, 15 Nov: Insights as a news front page for a weekly read, in the style of a news site's homepage, dark only, in the frame built on `claude/jobs-redesign` (`scripts/page_frame.py`). Three variations of one news model, all **reviewed** (one review pass: brief and wording, accessibility and keyboard, layout at each width; its findings were fixed, or kept and named as trade-offs in the notes). Hand-drawn HTML, not Superdesign drafts, so there are no canvas links. **Picked: B, Broadsheet** (15 Nov). A and C are kept as the options not taken. The open questions in the notes still stand for B, and the sections need a story type from the scorer before B can be built.

Shared by all three: the story card (company and source line, serif headline, two-line why it matters, relevance and the date found, Read the announcement, then Add to Watchlist, which becomes On your Watchlist, and Hide; no suggestions, no I did it); Top stories as a lead and four smaller, scrolling sideways on a phone with a count and arrows; four sections (Hiring and buying windows, Funding, Spin-offs and accelerators, Regulation and market news); Most relevant this week, 1 to 10; everything under 40 folded under More stories; and a story page with the summary, why it matters, every source, the company's facts, its Watchlist record and its roles on Jobs.

The notes frame, [insights_news_notes_1280_v1.png](insights/news/insights_news_notes_1280_v1.png) (1280x3971, with [HTML](insights/news/insights_news_notes_1280_v1.html)), holds each variation's rationale and trade-offs, keyboard behaviour, a contrast table and twelve open questions. The ones that block a build: nothing stores a story type today, so the sections need the scorer to return one; one story per event (grouping several reports) is new; no source or score band covers regulation; the Watchlist page does not exist yet; and removing I did it leaves the contact log and the 75-and-over drafts without a home.

**Decisions for building B, 15 Nov.** The open questions in the notes frame, settled:

1. Story type: the scorer returns one of eight types with each score (buying window, first hire, funding round,
   spin-off launch, accelerator entry, regulation, market news, other news), mapped onto the four sections. Recent
   stories are rescored once to fill it.
2. Grouping: reports are grouped automatically into one story when they share a company, fall within seven days
   and have similar headlines. The highest-scoring report leads, and a "Not the same story" link on the story
   page splits a wrong grouping.
3. Watchlist: Insights B is built first. Add to Watchlist saves the company straight away; until the Watchlist page
   exists, "On your Watchlist" links to the existing company page. The Watchlist page is the next piece of work.
4. Contact log and drafts: the story page holds the comment and connection-note drafts (with Copy) and a "Log a
   contact" control; both move to the Watchlist record when that page is built.
5. Week: a rolling seven days, as "fresh" works today.
6. Hidden stories: a closed "Hidden this week (N)" fold at the foot, each with Show again.
7. Card summary: the scorer also writes a one-sentence summary of about 90 characters for cards; the full
   why-it-matters line stays on the story page. It comes with the story-type change.
8. Regulation and market news: the section stays. Regulator and standards sources are added, with a score band
   for rule changes that affect device software; until they land the section reads "Nothing this week".
9. Serif: IBM Plex Serif is served from the dashboard itself, with Georgia as the fallback, and recorded as an
   approved exception for Insights headlines in the design system.

Two smaller points taken without a question: the scorer writes the buying-window headline along with the type and
summary (the frames build it from the role title), and dates stay as the day a story was found ("Found 12 Nov"),
since the publication date is not stored.
