# Design hand-off, round 1

Every frame on the Superdesign canvas [MedTech Radar redesign, round 1](https://superdesign.dev/teams/2140dc78-b239-499a-ac98-8022c63d00c3/projects/0db15a19-3516-4453-a7e9-67ecc19e8823), exported on 14 Nov 2026,
grouped by page and then by variation. A variation that covers both Prepare application and CV has its screens
split between those two folders under the same name.

**Files.** Each frame has three files side by side:

- a PNG of the whole page at the frame's width, full height (frames taller than Superdesign's 10,000px limit
  were taken whole from their preview links). App screens are drawn at a fixed viewport, as on the canvas, so
  content further down a scrolling pane is in the HTML, not the PNG;
- the draft's HTML, as Superdesign stores it (older versions: the exact file that was imported as that version);
- the CSS the page renders with: its own `<style>` blocks, then the rules the Tailwind CDN generated (the two
  blocks Superdesign's preview host injects are left out).

**Names.** `<page>_<variation>_<view>_v<version>`. The view is the width (`2560`, `1280`, `phone`, and `1440` for
round one) or `states` for a board of states, with a qualifier where a page has more than one frame at a width
(`2560-advert-read`, `1280-applied`, `phone-list`, `1280-company`). Shared pages are `shared_<what>_<width>_v<version>`.

**Status.** final: the agreed design. reviewed: drawn, checked by reviewers and fixed (or, for round one's
before frame, today's page used as the reviewed baseline), waiting for a pick. unreviewed: drawn and
self-checked by its author only (Home, Insights and the watchlist skipped their review round; round one's first
drafts came before its audit). **Current** marks the latest version of a frame, which is what the canvas shows;
older versions sit beside it. Two Jobs frames grew taller, so their v2 is a separate draft on the canvas.

**Counts.** 99 frames exported (297 files). 4 older versions could
not be exported; they are listed under jobs with the reason. All sample data is fictional. Round one's
direction notes address the owner by first name.

version after it. Restoring them would mean reverting the drafts on the canvas, which was not done.
