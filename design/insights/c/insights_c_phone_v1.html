Annotations

Insights, variation C, Signals table. A typical day, 13 Nov 09:14, at insights?view=signal&bar=40&state=open&sort=relevance. At 1280 the foot of the table is below the fold, so marker 10 shows at 2560 and on the phone.

  1. 1

    View switch

    By signal and By company are two views of the same signals. This one has a row for each event; By company has a row for each company and is the watchlist, so both pages read one set of data.

  2. 2

    The bar, a chip

    40 and over is selected when the page opens, so the 85 open signals below 40 stay out of the table. Clearing it brings them into the same table, and every filter is kept in the address.

  3. 3

    Type, Region and State

    Facet chips that narrow the table. State starts at Open, so done and dismissed rows are one choice away; Region reads a field that is often a guess for a company first met in the news.

  4. 4

    Watcher line

    When the signal watcher last ran and runs next, how many of the 26 sources it read, and whether the phone push is armed. When something fails it becomes a notice that names each failed source; the full list is folded at the foot.

  5. 5

    Scorer note

    One line above the table says whose words the rows are: the scorer's, from one article each, with relevance in bands. It sits over the columns so that a 74 is not read as more exact than it is.

  6. 6

    Column heads

    Relevance, Type, Company, Found and State sort, and the table opens on Relevance, high to low, so anything urgent is the first row. Under 1200px Type drops out; on the phone the headline cell carries the figure, the type, company and date, and the next move.

  7. 7

    Urgent row

    Hartwell Infusion Ltd, relevance 90: the one open signal at 75 or over. It comes first, tagged Urgent with a mark and the word, and opens itself; when several are urgent, each is tagged and none opens, so none pushes another down.

  8. 8

    Open row

    A row opens in place: why it matters, the next move in full, what the relevance rests on, each source that reported the event, the company's thread and whether the phone was told.

  9. 9

    Done and Dismiss

    The line beside the two buttons says what each writes: Done logs the next move as carried out and a contact for the company; Dismiss hides the row and keeps it from the digest and the phone. Both come back from the State chip or Undo last.

  10. 10

    Below the bar

    The table ends with the way to the 85 open signals below 40, so nothing is hidden without a route back to it.
