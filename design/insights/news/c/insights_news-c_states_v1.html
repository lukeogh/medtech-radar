Insights, variation C, Edition: states

The same front page in three kinds of week, each as the top of the page at 1280 and on a phone, then the story card in each of its states. Sample data is invented; “now” is Friday 13 Nov 2026, 09:14.

A quiet week

  • Nothing this week scored 40 or more, which happened in six weeks of sixteen so far.
  • No lead is invented. A plain note says the week was quiet, how many stories came in and the highest score, and points to More stories.
  • Up to three stories from earlier weeks stand in the Top stories place, under a heading that says so.
  • The sections collapse to one line instead of four empty headings. Most relevant still ranks the week, and says that everything in it is under 40.

A busy week

  • Back after a week away: 61 stories, 32 at 40 or more, one event reported by three sources.
  • Top stories does not grow with the week. The three reports of the Selwick Photonics spin-off are one story, with “and 2 more sources” on its source line; every report is on its story page.
  • Sections grow; earlier weeks stay as a short list with a link to the rest of the section.
  • 29 stories under 40 are folded into More stories at the foot, one button away.

The watcher failing

  • The watcher last finished 22 hours ago (the limit is 7 hours) and 7 of 26 sources could not be read.
  • A notice under the page heading says so in words, with an icon as well as colour, and links to what the watcher reported. The heading's date line changes to “stories as of” with the age.
  • Stories already on file stay readable and usable. Nothing pretends to be current.
  • The coverage figure in the heading drops to 19 of 26 sources answering.

The story card

Cards drawn at the width of a section grid at 1280. The toasts are drawn in place; on the page they sit at the foot of the window. The cards here are pictures of states and do not respond.
