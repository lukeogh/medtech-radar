    Watcher line

    The first band, drawn even when it is empty, so the rare urgent signal is the first row on the page. Its header says whether the phone push is armed, since 75 is also the line for the phone.

  3. 3

    Signal row

    One row per event: the headline, the relevance and the day it was found, then the company and the type, then why it matters in the scorer's words. An event reported by several sources stays one row and says “Reported by 3 sources”.

  4. 4

    Worth a look, 40 to 74

    The bar is 40, the scorer's own line under which an item is not a signal. This week's signals come first, then the 4 highest from earlier weeks that are still open and a link to the rest.

  5. 5

    Below the bar

    Everything under 40 is one count and one link, 85 today, so it never fills the list but stays one step away. Under it, Done and dismissed holds the undo for both actions, and Sources holds each source's last read.

  6. 6

    Actions

    Done and Dismiss write, and the line under them says what each does and where it is undone; Open the source leaves Radar in a new tab and Open the company goes to its record. On a phone they sit under the urgent row.

  7. 7

  8. 8
