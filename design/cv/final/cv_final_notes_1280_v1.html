CV, final: notes

The CV page drawn as a proposal for sign-off, on Friday 13 Nov 2026 at 09:14, from variation C (Margin notes) and the owner’s five requirements, after one review round. Frames: 2560, 1280 and phone with the active master open; 1280-preferences; 1280-overflow; the states board, where anything after 09:14 is labelled a later visit. Everything is invented: Alex Rennie, the employers (Corbel Medical, Linwood Infusion, Tarrow Surgical, Wexcombe Health, Pellam Diagnostics, Ardent Cardiac, Fairhurst Instruments), Brackley Surgical Ltd and the two universities (Kennet University, University of Harrowden). The master agrees with the Prepare application page’s tailored copy, which is made from it: the copy cuts master line 11, rewords line 13 (its 12) and adds its line 29, so from line 30 on both number alike. The line numbers are main’s: the drawing lays the CV out with scripts/cv_doc.render_lines and checks every number against it.

Rationale

The CV is the page. One A4 column in the middle of the canvas, a dark raised sheet in the frame’s surface colour, edited where it sits: hover or Tab to a section or entry and its outline appears; Enter or a click opens its lines as fields, with a small toolbar for that entry. That is the Enhancv pattern in Radar’s own tokens, type and words.

The toolbar above the sheet holds what acts on the whole CV, in one row at every state: Add section, Rearrange, Design, Undo and Redo (labelled icon buttons), then the save state, with Discard and the filled Save version beside it only while there is something to save. Line numbers moved into Design. The toolbar sticks to the top while the sheet scrolls, so it is there while page 2 is edited; the page keeps 72px of scroll padding so it never covers a focused line.

Everything about the CV that is not its text sits beside it: the masters, with the active one named as what the scorer reads, then History, Preferences and Word import as tabs, then the keys.

A change’s cost is said before it is spent. After a save, the notice above the page counts the roles still scored on an earlier version, and its button carries the count and the cost (“Rescore 38 roles, about 40 to 70 cents”); pressing it opens a confirm under the notice that says them again with what is left out, then the run and the result show in the same place. Preferences say plainly that changing them changes how roles are scored, and a saved change says how many roles it would move.

Breakpoints

One markup for every width; the layout is CSS media queries.

  • Under 768: one column: masters, the CV, the tabs (the keys panel is hidden, as a phone has no keys). The sheet reflows to the screen with a narrow number gutter, each section gets an Edit button, and the toolbar keeps Undo, Redo and a line numbers switch.
  • 768 to 1279 (so at 1024): the CV across the full width, the A4 sheet at 100% from 842px and at 90% below it; the masters and keys in one column and the tabs in another underneath.
  • 1280 to 1919 (so at 1600): the CV beside a 380px column of masters, tabs and keys; at 1600 the sheet sits centred in about 1150px of canvas.
  • From 1920 (so at 2560): three columns, masters and keys 400px on the left, the tabs 480px on the right, so the sheet is centred on the screen.

From variation C, and what changed

  • Kept: the dark sheet; one page column with tabs for History, Preferences and Import; the save state in words; the earlier-CV notice with an in-page confirm; append-only History with View; an entry’s controls (Add bullet, Add entry below, Move up, Move down, Delete) for one entry at a time; an imported version shown in the editor’s layout before it is trusted.
  • Changed: no Paper preview; a horizontal sticky toolbar with Add section, Rearrange and Design (C had none of the three); two named masters with Make active; Preferences edited here and saying what a change does, where C showed them read only; line numbers on by default; the page counts its A4 pages and says when it runs past two; the import names its guesses and lets you choose where it goes; one Rescore run reaches every affected role, and its button carries the count and the cost.

Keyboard

  • Order: skip link, bar, the earlier-CV notice and its Rescore button, then by layout: at 1280 the toolbar, the sheet, the masters, the tabs and the keys; at 2560 the masters first; under 768 the masters, the toolbar, the sheet and the tabs.
  • The toolbar and the tabs are one Tab stop each, with Left and Right (and Home, End) inside. Tabs follow the ARIA tabs pattern, each panel focusable.
  • On the sheet every part the keys move between is a Tab stop with a name and a hint: the head (“Name and headline, lines 1 to 3. Enter to edit”), each section (“Summary, section, lines 5 to 15. Enter to edit”) and each entry. Enter opens it and focus goes to its first line; each line is then a field and a Tab stop, with the entry toolbar after them. Esc leaves the line for its entry or section, Esc again closes it. Alt Shift Up and Down move the section or entry only while it, not a line inside it, has focus. Ctrl S saves a version, Ctrl Z undoes, Ctrl Shift Z redoes; the buttons carry aria-keyshortcuts.
  • Single-key shortcuts: the CV page has none. Every key above goes with Ctrl or Alt, or moves focus, so none can fire while typing and no On/Off switch is needed here.
  • Rearrange, Design, Make active and the Word import are modal dialogs: focus goes in, is held there, Esc cancels and focus returns to the button that opened it. Add section is a menu (arrows, Enter, Esc). The Rescore button has aria-expanded and aria-controls for its confirm, which takes focus when it opens. Go to page 3 moves focus to the page itself (id page-3, tabindex -1).
  • Status changes (saved, rescoring, done, import read) are announced through polite live regions; the picked-up section in Rearrange is announced assertively with its position.

Accessibility

Every state says itself in words and with a shape: the Active tag has a tick, unsaved has a dot and the words, caution a triangle, failure a cross. The selected Design option is ticked as well as tinted. Location and Sector are real checkboxes in fieldsets. Headings run h1 (CV), h2 for each panel, h3 for the CV’s sections and h4 for its entries; the sheet’s pages are articles named “page 1 of 2”. Buttons are 32px, 44px under a coarse pointer. Tokens added

None. The round-one paper tokens are gone: the sheet is --surface with --text-1 to --text-3, an open line is --surface-sunken with --border-strong, and the open entry is outlined in --accent.

What the code does today, and where the brief and code differ

  • Masters. The brief names two masters; main has one chain of dated versions (cv-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS.md, cv_store._write_version) and one cv.active marker. Drawn as the brief says.
  • The earlier-CV flag. Scores carry cv_version, and rescore.py --stale-cv re-scores rows whose stamp differs from the active CV, capped at 25 a run and only rows with the pre-3 Aug combined figure. It skips acknowledged, closed and applied-for roles and roles scored on a full description, as drawn (Hartwell Infusion’s role 418, scored 81 on its full description at 09:06, is one). No count is shown before the run today and the confirm says “This spends real tokens.” Every run logs its tokens to the runs table.
  • Preferences. The scorer reads the whole preferences file in its prompt, so a change applies from the next score; nothing records which preferences a score used. The day-rate floor, title and keywords are editable on Home; day_rate_floor_gbp, salary_floor_gbp, cv_pass_score (70) and cv_floor_score (40) are labelled lines in config/profile/preferences.md; location and sector are prose. “Last changed 11 Nov” is not stored. The brief’s salary floor of £85,000 is drawn; the tracked template says 100000.
  • Pages. Nothing counts A4 pages today; cv_word writes one fixed template (Arial, 10.5 pt body, A4, one column) and Word breaks the pages. The drawn count is an estimate from those sizes.
  • Sections. cv_doc keeps sections in any order and allows sections beyond the four standard ones (Selected projects and Talks and writing on cv-software-lead.md), but the editor cannot add or reorder sections today.
  • Import. .docx, .md, .txt and .pdf up to 5 MB, extracted and confirmed (cv_store); the refusal messages drawn are today’s in plainer words. The run happens on the box, which the page does not say.
  • Not drawn. The Jobs rows’ “Scored on an earlier CV” mark (that page’s) and the 768 to 1279 layout, described under Breakpoints.

Open questions

Each with the choice drawn and the alternatives.

1. The sheet: dark or light paper (shared with Prepare application)

Drawn
Dark, as the owner asked: an A4-proportioned sheet in --surface, raised off the canvas, Arial at the Word file’s sizes, page breaks and the two-page limit shown, line numbers quiet in its margin.
Alternatives
Light paper, which is what prints and what a recruiter sees (it needs seven paper colours the frame lacks); or the dark sheet with a Paper preview switch as a middle way.

2. Named masters or dated versions

Drawn
Named masters, cv-medtech-quality.md (active) and cv-software-lead.md, each with its own dated versions in History. Make active moves the one active marker.
Alternatives
Today’s single master: every save is a dated cv-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS.md and the newest is active, so a second CV would be a version, not a master. Or named masters with no version history.

3. How far one Rescore reaches

Drawn
One confirmed run for all 38 roles whose score names an earlier version of the active master, unacknowledged, open, not applied for and not scored on a full description.
Alternatives
Today’s cap of 25 a press (rescore.py --stale-cv --cap 25), then a second press for the other 13; and today only roles that still hold the pre-3 Aug combined figure are reached, so every role scored since is skipped.

4. The cost figure (shared: US cents everywhere)

Drawn
A range in US cents on the button: “Rescore 38 roles, about 40 to 70 cents”. The cost note puts a card score at about $0.011 with the prompt cache warm and $0.018 cold, so n roles cost 1.1n to 1.8n cents: 42 to 68 for 38, 44 to 72 for 40, 7 to 11 for 6. A finished run reports what the runs table logged (“37 paid calls, 44 cents”).
Alternatives
One figure at the cold price (“up to 70 cents”); or pounds and pence, which the bill is not in.

5. Preferences edited here

Drawn
All of them are edited in the Preferences tab, with one Save: both floors, location, sector, the pass mark and the floor. Home keeps a line that links here.
Alternatives
Read only here and edited where they are today (the day-rate floor, title and keywords on Home; the rest in config/profile/preferences.md by hand); or only the floors and gates here, with location and sector left as prose.

6. What a preference change flags

Drawn
A count of the roles it would move, with Rescore and its cost, where Radar can count from what it stores (the floors, the pass mark and the floor); for location and sector it says it cannot tell and offers to rescore all open roles.
Alternatives
Re-judge the Rate and CV gates in code without a model call, which costs nothing; or no flag, as today, where a change applies to the next score only and nothing records which preferences a score was made with.

7. Over two pages

Drawn
A notice naming page 3’s first line and section, a dashed two-page limit, and page 3 outlined, with the count in the toolbar and on the master’s row. Saving is still allowed.
Alternatives
Refuse to save over two pages; or show the page count only in Design. Either way the count is an estimate from the Word template’s sizes: Word decides the real breaks.

8. How much Design changes

Drawn
Font (Arial, Calibri, Georgia), text size, spacing and margins, applied to the sheet and the Word file alike, all within one column with no tables or images; plus the on-screen line numbers switch.
Alternatives
Spacing and margins only; or no Design, keeping today’s single template (Arial 10.5 pt), with the line numbers switch back in the toolbar.

9. Rearrange

Drawn
A dialog list with pick up, arrows and put down, and Move up and Move down named for each section; no dragging.
Alternatives
Drag as well as keys; or no mode at all, only Move up and Move down on each section’s own toolbar (variation C).

10. What a Word import does

Drawn
Read, show the differences, then choose: save as the next version of the open master, save as a new master, or discard.
Alternatives
Today’s Confirm, make it active, which makes the read text the active master at once.

11. Line numbers

Drawn
On, quiet, in the sheet’s margin, with a switch in Design (in the toolbar on a phone). Blank lines are numbered but show nothing, so the numbers skip; a number moves when a line is added above.
Alternatives
Off by default (variation C); or a stable number for each line, which is not built.

12. Editing on a phone

Drawn
Read-mostly: the CV reflowed, Edit beside each section heading opening its lines as fields, Undo and Redo; no Add section, Rearrange or Design.
Alternatives
Read only on a phone; or the whole editor, which a 390px screen makes cramped.

13. Undo and the Save button

Drawn
Undo and Redo cover unsaved edits, with Discard beside a filled Save version that shows only while there is something to save. A saved version is gone back to from History.
Alternatives
Undo across saves; or today’s Save button always enabled.
