Annotations

Address: /cv. Variation C, Margin notes. The master CV is saved and no score is stale; the Corbel Medical entry is in focus with its heading being edited. The CV, its employers and the roles are invented.

  1. 1

    Toolbar

    Save as a new version, Undo, Redo and the switches, as named buttons that stay put while the CV scrolls. On a phone they are a row of larger buttons above the CV, without Paper preview.

  2. 2

    Save state

    Saved or Unsaved changes, in words beside the CV's name, with the version file the scorer reads. A save makes a new version, so nothing is ever overwritten.

  3. 3

    Line numbers

    A switch, off by default, that shows the number of every line with words on it in the left margin. Blank lines are numbered too and show none, so the numbers skip. Numbers are positions, so they move when a line is added above.

  4. 4

    Dark sheet

    The page is drawn in the app's dark tokens with light ink, so it does not glare in a dark room. Paper preview in the toolbar shows it white and without margin numbers, as the Word file prints. The cost: what is on screen is not literally what prints until Paper preview is on, and under the Light appearance the sheet stays dark inside a light app.

  5. 5

    Numbers beside cited lines

    The 20 lines that asks cite show their numbers without the switch, so you can see which lines a score leans on before you change them.

  6. 6

    Entry in focus

    A click or a tap makes a line a field in place, so the CV is edited where it will print. The entry's controls show while it has the pointer or the focus: Add bullet, Add entry below, Move up, Move down, Delete. They sit in the sheet's margin, or under the heading on a phone, so none covers the text.

  7. 7

    Section headings

    A section heading shows its own controls on hover and focus: Add entry, Add line, Move up and Move down. That is how sections are reordered, with no mode to enter.

  8. 8

    One page column

    Page 2 follows page 1 in one column, broken where Word breaks it. On a phone the CV is reflowed for reading, and a rule marks where page 2 starts.

  9. 9

    History, Preferences, Import

    Three tabs share one column, beside the sheet on a desktop and under the CV on a phone, so the sheet keeps its full width whichever is open. Each tab is a link with its own address. At 2560 one tab at a time leaves most of this column empty: that is the cost of keeping the three in one column.

  10. 10

    Scoring preferences

    Read only, with when they last changed and the gate each one feeds, so a score can be checked against what it was judged on. The day-rate floor is edited on Home, the rest in the preferences file.
