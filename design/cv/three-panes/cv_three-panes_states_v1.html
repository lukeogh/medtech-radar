Summary

Software quality and regulatory leader for medical device software, 17 years in industry.

Fractional and interim roles since 2021: software quality, IEC 62304 and audit readiness.

Has taken three devices through notified body audits and one through FDA 510(k) clearance.

Works across embedded firmware, IVD analysers and software as a medical device.

Builds small, steady quality teams and leaves processes engineers will actually use.

Based in Surrey; open to hybrid roles in the south east and remote roles across the UK.

Selected outcomes across roles:

  • One FDA 510(k) clearance with software as a key part of the submission.

  • Two quality teams built from scratch, both still in place.

  • Release cycles cut by half at two companies without loosening controls.

Experience

Interim Head of Software Quality, Corbel Medical (Class IIb ventilators), 2024 to 2026

  • Brought the ventilator software file to audit readiness in seven months.

  • Led a team of seven software test and quality engineers across two sites.

  • Rewrote the software development plan and the software maintenance plan.

  • Closed 41 software CAPAs, 12 of them from the previous notified body audit.

  • Introduced weekly quality reviews with the embedded engineering leads.

  • Held two releases until every open anomaly had a documented risk assessment.

  • Recruited three permanent engineers and handed the team to a permanent head of quality.

Fractional Software Quality Lead, Linwood Infusion (syringe drivers), 2022 to 2024

  • Led IEC 62304 class C software for a Class IIb pump, from planning to release.

  • Owned the software lifecycle procedures and trained 30 engineers on them.

  • Prepared the software technical file for the notified body audit; no major findings.

  • Ran ISO 14971 risk management for software hazards and wrote the risk management report.

  • Set the verification and validation strategy for embedded firmware, including HIL rigs.

  • Brought MISRA C compliance and static analysis into the build, with a deviation register.

  • Set up traceability from user needs to verification in Jama.

Fractional Head of Software Quality, Tarrow Surgical (powered instruments), 2023 to 2024

  • Set up the software quality function for a 20-person firmware team before MDR certification.

  • Wrote the software verification plan and reviewed every protocol before it ran.

  • Audited two contract software suppliers and agreed their corrective actions.

  • Coached two engineers into software quality lead roles before handing over.

Fractional Regulatory Software Lead, Wexcombe Health (SaMD triage app), 2021 to 2023

  • Took a Class IIa triage app through UKCA marking and its first surveillance audit.

  • Fitted IEC 62304 class B records into the team's existing Jira and Git workflow.

  • Ran the clinical safety case under DCB0129 with the clinical safety officer.

  • Cut the regulatory review of a release from two weeks to two days.
