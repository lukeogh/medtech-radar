How the pages link

Caution: Unreviewed. The links below are read from screens that were drawn and self-checked by their authors; the review and fix round was skipped this time, so expect small faults.

Seven kinds of page: five in the navigation, Home, Jobs, Insights, Watchlist and CV, and two that come one per item, a company record for each company and Prepare application for each role. Today nothing inside a page has an address, so every link lands on the top of a page, and the watchlist has no page at all; every route that needs either is a proposal and is marked so. Every company and role named here is invented.

The shared navigation

Every page carries the same five entries, Home, Jobs, Insights, Watchlist and CV, and marks the current one with the accent bar. Under 768px the entries give way to Menu and the app bar names the page. The keyboard map of Jobs round 2 adds G then H, J, I, W or C to go to a page. Today the navigation holds Home, Jobs and Insights, and CV appears only on a Prepare application page.

Links between items

Each arrow stands for every link from one page to another in that direction, named by its words; the table under the drawing lists them one by one. A solid line marked Today is a link the product has now; a dashed line marked Proposal is one the screens propose. The dashed box is a page that does not exist today.

Routes

From, to, the link’s words, where it lands, and which screens draw it. No route from Home, Insights or the watchlist goes to Prepare application, which is reached only from a role on Jobs. Links that leave Radar, to the advert, the announcement or the workflow scheduler, open in a new tab and are left out.

Where the routes disagree

  • Settings: all three Home variations move the scorer fields and “Add a job source” to the CV page, while the CV page drafts drawn so far say the day-rate floor is edited on Home and link back there. One of the two has to change, whichever variations are chosen.
  • The watchlist: Insights C makes its By company view the watchlist, while the three watchlist variations draw it as a page of its own. If Insights C is chosen, the Watchlist entry would open that view.
  • Back links on a company record: the spec asks for “Back to Insights” or “Back to Jobs” when the record was reached from there, and every drawn record starts with “Back to Watchlist”.
  • A thread on Home C opens in Home’s own pane, and its link on to the watchlist is described in the author’s report but not drawn.
