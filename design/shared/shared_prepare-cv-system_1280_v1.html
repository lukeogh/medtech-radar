Prepare application and CV: system sheet

One navigation, one type scale, one spacing scale, one set of colour tokens and one set of components, shared with every page; then what the Prepare application and CV pages add: the CV canvas and its parts, the three papers a sheet can take, and the sheet's measurements beside the Word template's. Drawn dark; the light values sit beside the dark. These pages go beyond the shared rules in three ways: the sheet alone is set in Arial, as the Word file is; a status tint also sits behind a cited line on the sheet, not only inside a tag; and the variations change layout at widths of their own as well as at 1800, 1280, 1024 and 768px (A at 2400, 1440 and 1240px on the Prepare page and at 2100 and 1152px on the CV page, B at 2400 and 2200px, C at 2360px).
