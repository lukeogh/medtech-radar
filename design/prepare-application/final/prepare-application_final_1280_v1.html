Read from the advert 13 Nov 09:06, 2,964 characters, scored once for this version. Asks underlined: solid met, dashed partly, dotted gap, each with its mark and number.

About us

Hartwell Infusion makes volumetric and syringe infusion pumps for hospital wards and home care. Our pumps are Class IIb devices, CE and UKCA marked, and run on embedded firmware that we design, verify and maintain in house. We are 140 people at our Guildford site. In October we closed a Series C to widen the range and to prepare our first FDA 510(k) submission, planned for 2027. Software is now the largest part of each new pump, and we want a quality lead who has done this before.

The role

You will own the IEC 62304 software development lifecycle for our class C pump firmware.✓ A1, Met, show its note You will be the person our engineers, our notified body and our auditors turn to on software quality. In your first three months you will prepare the software technical file ahead of our notified body audit in Q1.✓ A2, Met, show its note After that you will set up the processes we need for the US submission. You will also work with our contract manufacturer in Swindon on the design transfer of each firmware release.✗ A3, Gap, not on the CV, show its note You will report to the VP Quality and work closely with the Head of Embedded Engineering, whose team of 22 writes the firmware.

What you will do

  • Act as internal auditor for software processes under ISO 13485.✓ A4, Met, show its note
  • Lead a team of six software test and quality engineers.✓ A5, Met, show its note
  • Run ISO 14971 risk management for software hazards, from hazard analysis to the risk management report.✓ A6, Met, show its note
  • Set the verification and validation strategy for embedded firmware, including hardware-in-the-loop testing.✓ A7, Met, show its note
  • Maintain SOUP and cybersecurity records under IEC 81001-5-1.◐ A8, Partly, show its note
  • Keep MISRA C and static analysis in a regulated codebase under control, with clear rules on deviations.✓ A9, Met, show its note
  • Own traceability in Polarion, from user needs to verification results.◐ A10, Partly, show its note
  • Present software quality status to the leadership team each month.◐ A11, Partly, show its note

What you will bring

  • Software quality leadership on Class IIb or Class III devices.✓ A12, Met, show its note
  • Experience of FDA 510(k) submissions.✓ A13, Met, show its note
  • Working knowledge of IEC 60601-1 and IEC 62366-1.◐ A14, Partly, show its note
  • A degree in engineering or a physical science.✓ A15, Met, show its note
  • Confidence with auditors, notified bodies and engineering teams alike.✓ A16, Met, show its note
  • Clear written English; you will write much of the technical file.✓ A17, Met, show its note
  • Desirable: infusion pumps under IEC 60601-2-24.◐ A18, Partly, show its note
  • Desirable: an ambulatory or home-use infusion device.✗ A19, Gap, not on the CV, show its note

Working pattern and pay

Hybrid, two days a week at our Guildford site. The rest of the week is remote. This is a 6-month contract, with a view to extension. £650 to £750 a day, outside IR35. Start in January.

How to apply

Send your CV and a short note on a technical file you have prepared. No agencies, please.
