Prepare application: notes

The final Prepare application page, for the owner to sign off: Software Quality Lead, Class II infusion pumps at Hartwell Infusion Ltd, Friday 13 Nov 2026, 09:14. Frames: 2560, 1280, phone (390), 1280-before-fetch, the states board and these notes. Built on variation C, Margin notes, after one review round. Every name, company and figure is invented; costs are US cents, as in docs/cost-note.md.

Rationale

The page answers one question: is this CV good enough to send for this role, and if not, which lines need work? So the tailored CV is the widest column and each ask from the advert sits beside the CV line that evidences it, like a reviewer's comment: its mark in a shape and a word (✓ Met, ◐ Partly, ✗ Gap, not on the CV), the advert sentence it came from in quotes with where it is, and the cited CV line numbers as links. What decides the application, the score with its basis, the four gates and the rate line, sits in the rail at 2560 and in a band above the sheet at 1280. The page never spends on opening: the fetch is a button that names its cost first.

What came from C, and what changed

Kept from C, Margin notes

  • Notes beside the first line they cite; the selected note and its lines share the tint; the line being edited opens in place with Move up, Move down and Delete outside the text.
  • The dark sheet in the app's tokens; cited line numbers shown, others hidden.
  • Asks with no line listed first; "was partly" and "was missing" tags after a rescore.
  • The advert at 2560 with each ask underlined in its sentence, solid, dashed or dotted, and a mark after it.
  • Back to Jobs, the role's title and facts as the page header; Jobs current in the bar.

Changed

  • Three scores (card, full spec, this copy) instead of two, each with its basis, and "never written to Jobs" said on the copy's.
  • Each note quotes its advert sentence and names its place, so the citation reads at 1280 and on a phone, where the advert is folded.
  • Rescore role, Save CV and Download Word sit together in one bar that sticks through the whole CV, at the top on a desktop and at the foot on a phone, with the save name under it. Scroll padding keeps it off a focused line.
  • Met notes fold to one line (mark, ask number, cited lines) and open on focus or click; Partly and Gap notes stay open. A note slides below its first line when the notes above need the room, so the sheet keeps its line spacing; its line numbers say where it belongs.
  • The line being edited is a textarea that grows with its text, at every width.
  • A cost-stating fetch button; C's "about 2p" corrected to about 2 cents (US), as the cost note gives it.
  • An Application panel: Preparing, Applied and the later stages, Mark applied (M) and the link to the applications board v2.
  • At 1280 a band of two columns: Score, Application and Rate line, then Gates with one line of reason each. At 2560 the rail and the advert scroll on their own. On a phone: the one-line score, Application, Rate line, Gates folded, the folded advert, then the gaps and the CV. The steps list of C is gone: the buttons say their own state.
  • The role is Software Quality Lead (the brief's), contract 6 months, £650 to £750 a day, so the rate gate shows a range judged on its top.

Where the drawing and main's code differ

  • Start on open. Main starts the fetch when the Jobs link is followed (go=1, Sec-Fetch-Site same-origin); drawn: always a button. The button's POST is still the one guarded write, one per press.
  • Advert sentences. Main stores each ask's words and CV lines (score_item.normalise_asks), not the sentence it came from.
  • Preparing. Main has no Preparing stage: Save CV stores saved_name and saved_at on the copy, and the applications board is a design (jobs/applied-board v2). Mark applied on main is the "Applied, sent it" button with the browser's confirm box.
  • Download. Main hides Download until a copy is saved; drawn shown and unavailable, with the reason.
  • Kept off the page. The scorer's model (claude-sonnet-4-6) and prompt (97f11cce), the 6,144-token reply cap behind "never more than about 9 cents", the HTTP 429 behind "LinkedIn asked Radar to slow down" and the page checker behind "a newer version of the advert". The notices' wording follows prepare_application.py on main: fetch_failed_text, _store_description, _store_score, COPY_BUSY, UNCHANGED_COPY.
  • Costs. US cents, from docs/cost-note.md: about 0.9 cent for the score, about 1.7 with the per-ask working, about 2 with the quotes. Rescore role is the same call on the copy.
  • Master line numbers. Before the fetch the copy is the master exactly as the CV page numbers it (cv_doc.render_lines): line 11 "Based in Reading" and line 13 "One US regulatory clearance". The tailored copy cuts line 11, rewords line 13 (its 12) and adds its 29.
  • Card score. Main replaces the card score in place (it goes to score_history); the page reads it back from the history to show it beside the spec score.

Keyboard and screen readers

TabIn the order drawn: Back to Jobs; Score, Application, Rate line, Gates; the job description (at 2560 its own scroll region); the CV bar; the gaps; then each CV line and its notes. Every control is a real button or link with the yellow focus ring (--focus). EnterOn a CV line (each is a button named "Line 23: ...", described by "Cited by A11. Enter edits the line."): opens it as a growing textarea with Move up, Move down and Delete. Esc closes it. MMark applied, as on the Jobs page. ZUndo the last move on the applications board, as on the board.

Single-key shortcuts (M, Z) can be turned off with the "Single-key shortcuts: On" switch beside the keys line in Application, and are suspended whenever focus is in a field. Each Met note is a button ("Met A16 lines 8, 50") that opens on focus or click; each open note has a "Show cited lines" button with the ask's number in hidden text, and the selected note's button carries aria-current. Notes are plain list items with their quote readable, no tab stop and no label hiding it. Citation links and Show it in the advert open the folded advert and move focus to the cited ask. Download Word uses aria-disabled with its reason as its description, so it stays in the tab order. Working, done and failed messages are status regions; the Mark applied dialog is modal, starts on its first button, keeps Tab inside, closes on Esc and returns focus. Headings: h1 the role; h2 Score, Application, Rate line, Gates, Job description, Tailored CV; h3 the CV's sections and h4 its entries. Meaning is never colour alone: every mark has a shape and a word, underlines differ in style, and the copy's figure has a dashed border and its words.

Open questions

1. Where the tailored score sits beside the card and spec scores

Drawn: Three figures in one row, Card 74, Full spec 81, This copy 86, the first faded and marked replaced, the second labelled "the role's score, shown on Jobs", the third dashed and labelled "this page only, never written to Jobs". The gates table then compares full spec and this copy column by column.

Alternatives: Two figures only, full spec and this copy, with the card score in the basis line (C's choice); or the tailored score in the CV's own header, away from the role's score, so it cannot be mistaken for it.

2. Does editing a CV line re-cite asks live, or only on Rescore

Drawn: Both, split by what is cheap: the cited lines are found again at once by their words (moved lines are followed, edited-away ones make the ask stale, in words) and the counts recount, as main's page script already does. The met, partly and gap marks change only on Rescore role, because judging them is the paid call.

Alternatives: Nothing moves until Rescore (simpler, but the notes then sit beside the wrong lines); or a free local guess at the marks as you type (fast, but a guess the scorer may not agree with).

3. What happens to a tailored CV after the master changes

Drawn: Nothing by itself. The copy keeps its text and its score, the page says the master changed and when, and Start again from master takes the new one. The saved copy stays as saved (states board, 12).

Alternatives: Offer the master's changed lines one by one to copy across; or rebuild an unsaved copy from the new master automatically and keep only saved copies fixed.

4. The cost-first button against main's automatic start

Drawn: The page never spends on opening. One button, "About 2 cents: Fetch and score the full description", with the breakdown and the 6,144-token ceiling under it. On main, following the Jobs link (go=1 with Sec-Fetch-Site same-origin) starts the fetch as the page opens, and only other routes get a button.

Alternatives: Keep main's start-on-open and put the cost on the Jobs row's Prepare application button instead; or start on open only for Top prospects and show the button for the rest.

5. Which advert sentence an ask came from

Drawn: Each note quotes its sentence and names where it is ("What you will do, item 8"), and at 2560 the advert column underlines it. Main's per-ask working stores the ask's words and its CV lines, not the sentence: the scorer would return the sentence too, kept only if it is found word for word in the stored description, as the reasons' quotes are.

Alternatives: Match the ask's words to the description in code, with no prompt change (cheaper, sometimes wrong); or show the ask's words only and drop the advert column.

6. Where the gaps sit

Drawn: First, in a dashed block above the sheet, each with its sentence and a reason, and in the advert with a dotted underline and a cross. They have no CV line to stand beside.

Alternatives: At the foot of the notes column; or as a note beside the CV section where the evidence would belong (a guess the scorer does not make).

7. Save CV's name

Drawn: The full job title, "CV - Alex Rennie - Hartwell Infusion Ltd - Software Quality Lead, Class II infusion pumps", as main's cv_doc.saved_name makes it (the title kept up to 79 characters) and as the applications board v2 names its files, for example "CV - Alex Rennie - Quayle Health - Head of Regulatory Software, SaMD.docx".

Alternatives: The short form cut at the title's first comma ("... - Software Quality Lead"), which the brief first gave; it needs a code change and the board's names redrawn.

8. A dark or a light sheet

Drawn: Dark, as on the CV page: an A4-proportioned raised sheet in the frame's surface tokens, page breaks shown, line numbers quiet. The owner asked for dark only.

Alternatives: Light paper, which is what prints and what Download Word gives; or a Paper preview switch on the dark page as a middle option.

9. Notes that do not fit beside their line

Drawn: Met notes fold to one line, and a note slides down below its first line when the notes above need the room, so the sheet's lines stay evenly spaced.

Alternatives: Keep every note level with its line and let the sheet open gaps (C's choice, about 100px between lines 27 and 35); or show only the selected line's notes.

10. Mark applied without a saved CV

Drawn: Allowed, through the dialog (states board, 9), which offers Save CV first; the board then says no CV was saved, as main's applied banner does.

Alternatives: Mark applied unavailable until something is saved; or save silently on Mark applied.
