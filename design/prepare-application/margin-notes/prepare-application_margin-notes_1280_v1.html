About us

Hartwell Infusion makes volumetric and syringe infusion pumps for hospital wards and home care. Our pumps are Class IIb devices, CE and UKCA marked, and run on embedded firmware that we design, verify and maintain in house. We are 140 people across engineering, quality, manufacturing and clinical support, based at our Guildford site. The current range is the V2 volumetric pump and the S1 syringe driver, used in more than 300 NHS and private hospitals. We ship two firmware releases a year for each pump, and a third product, an ambulatory pump for home infusion, is in design. In October we closed a £32m Series C to widen the range and to prepare our first FDA 510(k) submission, planned for 2027. Software is now the largest part of each new pump, and we want a quality lead who has done this before.

The role

You will own the IEC 62304 software development lifecycle for our class C pump firmware., Met You will be the person our engineers, our notified body and our auditors turn to on software quality. In your first three months you will prepare the software technical file ahead of our notified body audit in Q1., Met After that you will set up the processes we need for the US submission and help us hire into the team. You will report to the VP Quality and work closely with the Head of Embedded Engineering, whose team of 22 writes the firmware. Our software quality team is six people today, and we plan to add two more in 2027.

What you will do

  • Act as internal auditor for software processes under ISO 13485., Met
  • Lead a team of six software test and quality engineers., Met
  • Run ISO 14971 risk management for software hazards, from hazard analysis to the risk management report., Met
  • Set the verification and validation strategy for embedded firmware, including hardware-in-the-loop testing., Met
  • Maintain SOUP and cybersecurity records under IEC 81001-5-1., Partly
  • Keep MISRA C and static analysis in a regulated codebase under control, with clear rules on deviations., Met
  • Own traceability in Polarion, from user needs to verification results., Partly
  • Present software quality status to the leadership team each month., Partly, selected

What you will bring

  • Software quality leadership on Class IIb or Class III devices., Met
  • Experience of FDA 510(k) submissions., Met
  • Working knowledge of IEC 60601-1 and IEC 62366-1., Partly
  • A degree in engineering or a physical science., Met
  • Confidence with auditors, notified bodies and engineering teams alike., Met
  • Clear written English; you will write much of the technical file., Met
  • Desirable: infusion pumps under IEC 60601-2-24., Partly
  • Desirable: an ISO 13485 lead auditor certificate., Met

Your first year

  • Months 1 to 3: the software technical file and the notified body audit.
  • Months 4 to 6: a gap assessment against what the FDA will expect in our 510(k).
  • Months 7 to 12: the software plan for the ambulatory pump, and two new hires.

Working pattern and pay

Hybrid, two days a week at our Guildford site. The rest of the week is remote, with occasional travel to our contract manufacturer in Swindon. This is a 12-month contract with a view to extension. Day rate £700–£750, outside IR35. Start in January.

How to apply

Send your CV and a short note on a technical file you have prepared. We will reply to every applicant within ten working days. No agencies, please.
