Home r2 A, stacked band: a typical day, 13 Nov 09:14. At 2560 and 1280 the band is four across; at 2560 the list fills the rest of the frame, at 1280 the page scrolls to it; on a phone everything stacks. Proposals drawn here: weekly Top prospect history, application stages and Waiting on you, applications due today in the list, the per-job health words and limits, and Done on Home items. Every company, person and product is invented.

  1. 1

    Welcome strip

    The date, the greeting by the time of day (Good morning before 12:00, Good afternoon to 18:00, then Good evening), the status sentence, whose counts are the groups of the list below, and the as-of line with the one link to Settings, on the CV page. From 1024px it is one strip: date and greeting at the left, the sentence and the as-of line beside them. On a quiet day a second line says what Radar read and when it next runs. The live page uses the owner's first name.

  2. 2

    Open roles: where do the open roles narrow?

    All open, Reading, One question away and Top prospects, the same counts as the Jobs tier tabs, with what is new. Each step opens Jobs at that tier. Bars are on a square-root scale so 8 shows beside 530.

  3. 3

    Week by week: is the flow picking up?

    New Top prospects (solid) and signals at 40 or more (hatched) for the last four weeks, this week so far, each row on its own scale, with the figure on every bar and one sentence comparing this week with last. Weekly Top prospect history is a proposal: Radar keeps none today.

  4. 4

    Applications: where do my applications stand?

    The six stages with their counts, each a link to its column, then what waits on the owner. The tile opens the full board on Jobs. The stages and the waiting items are a proposal.

  5. 5

    Radar's health: is Radar running as it should?

    The five jobs as lights, each a mark, a colour and a word (On time, Late, Stale, Off, Dry run) with one detail. The per-job words and limits are a proposal.

  6. 6

    Needs you today

    Round-1 Home C's list and detail, below the band. At 2560 it fills the rest of the screen. From 1024 to 1799 the page scrolls and this block is one screen high when it reaches the top, where scrolling snaps it; its list and detail then scroll inside it. To do and Done switch the list; Done is stored (a proposal), and Undo last puts the latest one back.

  7. 7

    Groups, most urgent first

    A Radar job that needs a look heads the list, then urgent signals, new Top prospects, new One question away roles, threads due, and applications due today or overdue (a proposal; a follow-up due tomorrow stays on the tile). A group with nothing in it is not drawn.

  8. 8

    Selected item

    The accent bar marks it; J and K move the selection. From 720px of room its detail is in the pane at the right; on a phone it opens under its own row.

  9. 9

    Open and Done
