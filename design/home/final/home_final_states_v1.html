Home final: the states

Four states of Home final, each a whole screen at 1280 drawn by the same function as home-final.html, then two of them on a phone. Friday 13 Nov 2026 at 09:14 (Times are UK time.) On every screen the five figures equal the status sentence's counts and the list's groups. On a quiet day the figures stay, each at 0 and dimmed, rather than going away: the welcome keeps its shape from day to day, each figure still opens the place where those items would arrive, and the zeros say the same as the sentence above them, so the calm comes from the words, not from a missing part. Radar's own faults are not figures: they are named in the strip and head the list. Every company, person and product is invented. Not stored by Radar today: application stages and next steps, the 21-day rule, weekly Top prospect history, per-job health limits.
